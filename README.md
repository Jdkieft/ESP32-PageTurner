# ESP32-PageTurner
ESP32 based page turner for Kobo eReaders and other devices


### Needed Supplies

- Seeed Studio xiao esp32-c6 
    - (the XIAO ESP32-C3 and XIAO ESP32-S3 can also be used)

- 1 Mechanical Keyboard Switch, It doesnt really matter which, it only has to be full size. The colour is entiliry depended on your preferences.
- 1 4mm mini Slide Switch with 3 pins
- 1 5mm Led, colour doesn't matter
- 1 TTP223B Touch Sensor
- Thin cable to solder everything together
- 2 220K Ohm 1/4 watt resistors
- 1 220 Ohm 1/4 Watt resistor
- 1 3.7v LiPo battery with the size of 9x26x32 mm
- some thin double sided tape
- 35 grams of pla filement
